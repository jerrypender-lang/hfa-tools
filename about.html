It all starts with one question

“How’s your health?”

Dr. Bell has spent his life meeting people where they are and asking that same simple question. For more than 40 years, he’s turned it into a calling.

A Columbia native and Charleston-based family physician, Dr. Bell founded Closing the Gap in Healthcare to share plainspoken, culturally rooted health information with communities often left out of the system. His message travels through short videos, church talks, barbershop conversations and civic gatherings across the state.

What started as a mission to address disparities in healthcare, particularly among African Americans and underserved communities, has grown into something bigger: helping people from all backgrounds understand what their health is telling them, so they can act early and live longer, stronger lives.

He’ll tell you his real reward is seeing someone walk away with clarity, confidence and a better grasp of their own care. He walks with a faith that has never wavered and a family that’s walked beside him every step of the way.
