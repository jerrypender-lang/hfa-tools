About Dr. Thaddeus J. Bell

Still trusted. Still speaking. Still serving.

Dr. Thaddeus Bell has spent his life meeting people where they are—barbershops, pulpits, front porches—asking the same simple question: “How’s your health?” For more than 40 years, he’s turned that question into a calling.

You can’t fix what you don’t understand.

A Columbia native and Charleston-based family physician, Dr. Bell founded Closing the Gap in Healthcare to share plainspoken, culturally rooted health information with communities often left out of the system. His message travels through short videos, church talks, barbershop conversations, and civic gatherings across the state.

What started as a mission to address disparities in healthcare—particularly among African Americans and underserved communities—has grown into something even bigger: helping more people, from all backgrounds, understand what their health is telling them, so they can take action early and live longer, stronger lives.

He has been honored with the Order of the Palmetto, the South Carolina Black Hall of Fame, and Physician of the Year—but he’ll tell you his real reward is seeing someone walk away with clarity, confidence, and a better grasp of their own care.

Now 81, he still sees patients. Still speaks. Still shows up. He walks with a faith that has never wavered and a family that’s walked beside him every step of the way.

In his words

“You can’t fix what you don’t understand. That’s what I’ve seen for decades—not in theory, but in real life.

We often discuss access to care, and it remains a crucial issue. But just as often, people delay care because they don’t recognize the signs.

It’s about understanding yourself—your numbers, your symptoms, your options. That’s where I come in.

Because the more you know, the better you live. And everybody deserves to live well.”

— Dr. Thaddeus Bell, Founder, Closing the Gap in Healthcare
