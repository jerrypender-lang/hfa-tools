    Your next appointment

    Walk in prepared,
    not panicked.

    • 01Write down what’s bothering you: when it started, how often it happens, and what makes it better or worse.
    • 02Bring every medicine you take, including vitamins and anything over the counter. The bottles themselves are best.
    • 03Pick your top one or two concerns. Say them first, before time runs out.
    • 01Ask: “What is my main problem?”
    • 02Ask: “What do I need to do?”
    • 03Ask: “Why is it important for me to do this?”
    • 04If a word doesn’t make sense, say so. “Can you say that a different way?” is always fair.

    Common questions

    Fair questions.

    What is the “knowledge gap”?

    It’s what happens when people don’t know what their symptoms mean, what their numbers tell them, or what questions to ask. That confusion can be dangerous, and it’s exactly what Dr. Bell helps people overcome.

    Is Closing the Gap still focused on underserved communities?

    Absolutely, but the message is broader. Everyone can benefit from a better understanding of health. Dr. Bell is making sure more people get that clarity.

    What about AI, Google, or TikTok health tips?

    Technology can be helpful, but it can’t replace your doctor. It can’t see your whole picture. Dr. Bell helps people understand what matters, what to ask, and when to trust real guidance over viral guesses.

    Is anything on this page medical advice?

    No. Everything here is general education to help you have a better conversation with your own doctor, who knows your history.

    Because the more you know, the better you live.
