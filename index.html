Culture and care go together.

Dr. Bell founded the Lowcountry Jazz Festival with his daughter, Tonisha. Every Labor Day weekend at the Charleston Gaillard Center, world-class jazz funds scholarships for future healthcare workers, and keeps Closing the Gap’s health education going.
