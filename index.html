Questions

Plain answers.

Is this a rebrand or a retirement?

Neither. Dr. Bell is expanding his message, not stepping back. The focus now is on helping more people understand their health clearly and act on it with confidence.

What is the “knowledge gap”?

It’s what happens when people don’t know what their symptoms mean, what their numbers tell them, or what questions to ask. That confusion can be dangerous, and that’s precisely what Dr. Bell helps people overcome.

Is Closing the Gap still focused on underserved communities?

Absolutely—but the message is broader. Everyone can benefit from a better understanding of health. Dr. Bell is making sure more people get that clarity.

Where can I find Dr. Bell’s health tips?

Right here on our Dr. Bell Says page, on your local radio, on YouTube, and at community events across South Carolina.

What about AI, Google, or TikTok health tips?

Technology can be helpful, but it can’t replace your doctor. It can’t see your whole picture. Dr. Bell helps people understand what matters, what to ask, and when to trust real guidance over viral guesses.
