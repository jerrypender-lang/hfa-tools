Best for event programs and professional settings

Dr. Thaddeus J. Bell is a family medicine physician in Charleston, South Carolina, and serves as Associate Dean for Diversity and Clinical Associate Professor at the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC). He is also the founder of Closing the Gap in Healthcare, a nonprofit platform delivering plainspoken, culturally grounded health education across the state. Dr. Bell also founded the Lowcountry Jazz Festival, a philanthropic event that funds scholarships for students in all six MUSC colleges.

Best for newsletters, videos, social media and radio

Dr. Thaddeus Bell helps people make sense of their health. As a family physician and founder of Closing the Gap in Healthcare, he’s dedicated to sharing clear, plainspoken health information, especially in communities where care and trust have historically been out of reach. You’ll find him in barbershops, churches and videos online, still showing up to help people understand what their health is telling them.

Best for church bulletins, civic honors and cultural events

Dr. Thaddeus J. Bell is a respected physician, educator and lifelong servant-leader in the South Carolina community. As the founder of Closing the Gap in Healthcare and the Lowcountry Jazz Festival, Dr. Bell has dedicated his life to making health make sense, especially for those historically left out of the conversation. Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Bell is a man of faith and family, a proud father and grandfather. His message remains clear: “Health literacy is the key to better health.”

Best for sponsor decks, festival booklets and funding appeals

Dr. Thaddeus J. Bell is the founder of the Lowcountry Jazz Festival, South Carolina’s premier jazz celebration, created to fund scholarships for students attending any of MUSC’s six colleges. In addition to producing this annual cultural event, Dr. Bell is a practicing family physician and founder of Closing the Gap in Healthcare, a nonprofit platform delivering plainspoken, culturally grounded health education through video series, barbershop outreach and community campaigns.
