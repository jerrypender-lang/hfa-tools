    The Lowcountry Jazz Festival.

    Founded in 2009 by Dr. Bell and his daughter Tonisha. Every Labor Day weekend at the Charleston Gaillard Center, world-class jazz funds scholarships for future healthcare workers and keeps Closing the Gap’s health education going.

    • Scholarships for students in any of MUSC’s six colleges
    • The main source of funding for Closing the Gap
    • A community health summit with Black Health Matters
